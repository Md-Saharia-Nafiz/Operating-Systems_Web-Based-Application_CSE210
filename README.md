
# Smart Blood Donation Management System

**Smart Blood Donation Management System is a web-based platform developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** to simplify blood donation and blood request management across Dhaka. It enables donors to register and track donation history, allows patients to request blood by area and hospital, manages donor eligibility with a 3-month donation interval, and provides an admin panel for complete system management.


## 📄Description :

**Smart Blood Donation Management System** is a web-based application developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to simplify and manage blood donation activities across Dhaka city. It allows users to register as blood donors, send blood requests, and find suitable donors based on blood groups and locations. The system provides an organized list of Dhaka's areas and hospitals, making it easier to locate blood requirements. Donor profiles maintain donation history, eligibility status, and the next available donation date, following a 90-day interval between donations. An admin panel manages donors, blood requests, user activities, and overall system operations. The application also includes a reporting and analytics system to identify areas with high blood demand and compare blood requirements across government and private hospitals. Its main goal is to make blood donation faster, more organized, accessible, and efficient while connecting blood donors with people in need.


## 💡 Key Concepts Used :

- **Donor Registration & Management :** Register donors and manage their blood groups, personal profiles, and donation history.
- **Blood Request System :** Allow users to submit blood requests and find suitable donors.
- **Location-Based Search :** Organize Dhaka city areas and hospitals to locate blood requirements easily.
- **Donation Eligibility Tracking :** Maintain a 90-day donation interval and automatically track the next eligible donation date.
- **User Profile Management :** Track donation records, donor information, and eligibility status through individual profiles.
- **Admin Management System :** Manage donors, blood requests, users, and overall application activities.
- **Report & Data Analytics :** Analyze blood demand by area and identify government and private hospitals with higher blood requirements.


## ✨ Features :

- **Donor Registration & Management :** Users can register as blood donors and manage their personal profiles and donation history.
- **Blood Request System :** Users can submit blood requests by selecting blood groups, locations, and hospitals.
- **Area & Hospital Management :** Organized lists of Dhaka city areas and hospitals (Government & Private) for easy blood searching and requesting.
- **90-Day Donation Eligibility :** Automatically tracks donation dates and determines when a donor becomes eligible to donate blood again after 90 days.
- **Donor Profile & History :** Displays previous donation records, personal information, and the next eligible donation date.
- **Admin Dashboard :** Provides centralized management of donors, blood requests, users, and overall system activities.
- **Reports & Analytics :** Analyzes blood demand by area and hospital, helping identify high-demand locations and compare government and private hospital requirements.


## 🔔 Purpose :

The purpose of the Smart Blood Donation Management System is to create a centralized platform that connects blood donors with people in need across Dhaka city. It simplifies donor registration, blood donation tracking, and blood request management through an organized list of areas and hospitals. The system maintains donor profiles, donation history, and 90-day donation eligibility to ensure proper donation intervals. An admin panel provides complete management of donors, requests, and system activities. Additionally, its reporting and analytics features identify high-demand areas and hospitals, helping improve blood donation management and ensure faster access to life-saving blood.

## ▶️ How to Run :

1. Clone or download the project repository from GitHub.
2. Extract the downloaded ZIP file (if downloaded).
3. Open the project folder in Visual Studio Code.
4. Make sure all project files and folders are properly organized.
5. Install the **Live Server** extension in Visual Studio Code.
6. Open the main `index.html` file.
7. Right-click on `index.html` and select **Open with Live Server**.
8. The application will automatically open in your default web browser.
9. Explore the available features, including donor registration, blood requests, donor profiles, and admin management.
10. Use the dashboard and reporting system to explore blood donation activities and analytics.
